1. Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Compliance: India DPDP Act & Global Standards
Data Minimization Principle: Zappora allows instant APK downloads and web gameplay without mandatory account registration.

Information We Collect

  • Public Visitors: You may browse and download applications without sharing personal data. Local preferences (such as dark mode, wishlist, and recently viewed apps) are stored locally in your browser's localStorage.
  • Registered Users & Developers: When you create an account or sign in via Google, we store your display name, chosen @username, and email address in Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore to enable verified reviews and app publishing.

How We Use Your Data

We use registered account details strictly to display your developer studio identity, attribute community ratings, send a one-time welcome confirmation email, and prevent platform abuse. We never sell or rent user email addresses to third parties.

Data Deletion Requests

Users and developers may request complete account and data deletion at any time by emailing zapporastore.official@gmail.com.

4. App Submission Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Technical Requirements

  • All Android packages must be valid .apk files hosted over a secure HTTPS URL (such as GitHub Releases) and under the 250MB size limit.
  • Developers should generate the package SHA-256 Integrity Checksum in the submission form so users can verify file authenticity.

Prohibited Content

  • Trojans, spyware, adware, rootkits, or hidden background crypto-miners.
  • Real-money gambling, unauthorized betting apps, or deceptive financial schemes.
  • Modified ("modded") or pirated versions of copyrighted commercial applications.

9. Security & APK Integrity Policy

Last Updated: November 2026
Transparent Security Disclosure: Zappora provides SHA-256 cryptographic checksum verification and manual moderator review, but does not claim automated server-side antivirus scanning unless explicitly linked via VirusTotal.

How Zappora Protects Users & Developers

  • SHA-256 Checksums: Every APK submitted through the Developer Console can have its SHA-256 hash computed in-browser and displayed publicly on the store page, plus a direct 1-click lookup link to VirusTotal.
  • Strict Firestore Rules: Database access is governed by least-privilege Firebase Security Rules, locking administrative operations to the verified Super Administrator UID.
